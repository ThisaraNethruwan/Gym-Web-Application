## 📋 Overview

FitZone is a comprehensive fitness center management system designed to transform traditional gym operations into a streamlined modern experience. This solution addresses the challenges of manual member management by providing an integrated platform that improves efficiency, enhances member engagement, and optimizes fitness center operations.

The project was developed as an individual university assignment over the course of one month, encompassing all development phases from initial planning and design to implementation and thorough testing.
